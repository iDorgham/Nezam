      <strong>❌ Modal-First Approaches</strong>
      <p style="color: #6b7280; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-top: 0.5rem;">Avoid intrusive overlays</p>
    </div>
    <div style="background: white; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid #ef4444;">
      <strong>❌ Gradient Text</strong>
      <p style="color: #6b7280; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-top: 0.5rem;">Skip decorative typography effects</p>
    </div>
    <div style="background: white; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid #ef4444;">
      <strong>❌ Glassmorphism</strong>
      <p style="color: #6b7280; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-top: 0.5rem;">No frosted UI effects</p>
    </div>
    <div style="background: white; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid #ef4444;">
      <strong>❌ Side-Stripe Borders</strong>
      <p style="color: #6b7280; font-size: 0.9rem; margin-top: 0.5rem;">Avoid decorative side borders</p>
    </div>
    <div style="background: white; padding: 1rem; border-radius: 8px; border-left: 3px solid #ef4444;">
